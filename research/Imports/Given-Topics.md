## 1. Real-Time Vietnamese-English Business Meeting Translator

**Sponsored by:** AI Singapore

### Challenge Overview

In this 2-day AI hackathon, teams are challenged to build a working prototype of a real-time translator for in-person business meetings between Vietnamese and Singaporean delegations. The solution must support **Vietnamese-to-English** and **English-to-Vietnamese** communication with **near real-time performance**, enabling both sides to communicate accurately, efficiently, and with a smooth user experience.

The expected outcome is a **working demo** that can be used live during the judging session, where judges and participants will engage in free-flow exchanges in both Vietnamese and English.

The prototype may run on a laptop, tablet, smartphone, or another small device assembled during the competition. The challenge does not prescribe whether translated output must be delivered as voice or text; either is acceptable, provided the result appears almost in real time and is practical for live meeting use.

### Core Requirements

- Build a functional prototype within the 2-day hackathon period.
- Support bidirectional translation between Vietnamese and English.
- Operate in a live, in-person business meeting setting.
- Be demonstrated live during the judging session through free-flow exchanges in Vietnamese and English between the judges and the participants.
- Deliver translations with strong communication accuracy and low perceived latency.
- Provide a user experience that is intuitive, efficient, and minimally disruptive to the meeting flow.
- Run on accessible computing hardware such as a laptop, tablet, smartphone, or a compact custom-built device.
- Teams may use AI assistants to help design, code, test, and refine their solution.

### Team Rules

Each competing team may consist of up to **three engineers and/or scientists**.

### Bonus Considerations

- **On-premise / Open models:** Bonus points for solutions built using open AI models that can be hosted on-premise, suitable for meetings involving confidential or sensitive information.
- **Edge deployment:** Bonus points for solutions deployable on edge devices.
- **Noise robustness:** Bonus points for working demos that remain effective in noisy environments and handle conversational turn-taking well.
- **Extensibility:** Recognition for designing extensible systems adaptable to additional language pairs, especially lower-resource languages.

### What Strong Solutions Should Demonstrate

Strong submissions should demonstrate a clear balance between **translation quality, responsiveness, usability, and deployability** in realistic business settings. Teams are encouraged to think beyond raw model performance and consider practical issues such as turn-taking, background noise, speaker changes, privacy, hardware constraints, and how their interface supports natural conversation flow.

### Judging Rubric

| Criterion | Suggested Weight | What Judges Should Look For |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------------------------|
| **Translation accuracy** | **30%** | How accurately the solution preserves meaning, intent, and key details in both Vietnamese-to-English and English-to-Vietnamese exchanges. |
| **Latency and responsiveness** | **20%** | How quickly the system produces output and whether it supports a natural, near real-time conversational flow. |
| **User experience and meeting flow** | **20%** | How intuitive, smooth, and minimally disruptive the interaction is during a live business-style conversation. |
| **Robustness in realistic conditions** | **15%** | How well the system handles turn-taking, speaker changes, and environmental noise during the live demo. |
| **Technical design and deployability** | **15%** | The soundness of the implementation, suitability for practical deployment, and clarity of the overall system design. |
| **Bonus: On-premise / Open models** | + | Solutions using open AI models that can be hosted on-premise. |
| **Bonus: Edge deployment** | + | Solutions deployable on edge devices. |
| **Bonus: Noise / turn-taking** | + | Effective performance in noisy environments and conversational turn-taking. |
| **Bonus: Extensibility** | + | Adaptability to additional language pairs, especially lower-resource languages. |

### Prize & Opportunity

- **Prize money** from AI Singapore
- **Sponsored 1- to 2-month visiting researcher experience** at the **AI Singapore office at Nanyang Technological University (NTU)**, Singapore

> This challenge invites participants to combine **speech, translation, systems engineering, and human-centered design** to create a tool that can make multilingual business communication more seamless, private, and inclusive.
