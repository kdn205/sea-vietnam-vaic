# Vietnam AI Innovation Challenge 2026 (VAIC 2026)

**Theme:** "Train"formation  
**Type:** 100% AI-native Hackathon  
**Dates:** 17–19 July 2026 (48 hours)  
**Venue:** FPT Tower, 10 Pham Van Bach, Cau Giay, Hanoi, Vietnam  

## Organisers

| Role | Organisation |
|------|-------------|
| Lead Co-Organiser | National Innovation Center (NIC) — Ministry of Planning & Investment |
| Global Partner | Meta |
| Operations | AI for Vietnam Foundation (AIV) |
| Academic Partner | Duy Tan University |
| Venue Host | FPT Corporation |

## Key Figures

- ~2,000 registrations, 300+ completed teams as of 13 July
- International participants included
- Total prize pool: >1.6 billion VND (~$68,000+)
- 8 challenge tracks across industry and social domains

## Strategic Context

- Part of the **Vietnam Innovation Challenge 2026** framework
- NIC officially launched the **International Artificial Intelligence Olympiad (IAIO) 2027** during the event — first time Vietnam hosts an international AI olympiad
- Talent pipeline aiming for IAIO 2027 and global AI competitiveness

## Structure

1. **8 Challenge Tracks** — real-world problem statements from enterprises
2. **48-hour Build Sprint** — teams develop AI-native prototypes with mentor guidance
3. **Judging & Awards** — multi-stakeholder jury evaluates on criteria
4. **Post-Hackathon Pathways** — piloting, national showcase (Vietnam Innovation Day), regional (NTU/AI Singapore), global (Silicon Valley)
