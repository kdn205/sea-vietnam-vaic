# Challenge Tracks — 8 Chủ Đề

All tracks are **real-world problem statements sourced from enterprises and public-sector needs**. Teams build AI-native prototypes (no pure algorithmic submissions — products must demonstrate real-world applicability).

---

## 1. Banking & Finance (Ngân hàng & Tài chính)

- **Description:** Apply AI to transform the financial industry — automating operations, risk management, and enhancing customer experience.
- **Sponsor / Partner:** SHB (Saigon-Hanoi Commercial Bank) — Diamond Sponsor
- **Special Prize:** Best Banking & Finance Solution ($5,000 cash, sponsored by SHB)

## 2. Healthcare (Y Tế & Sức Khỏe)

- **Description:** AI applications in healthcare — diagnostic support, operational optimisation, and improving medical service quality.
- **Potential areas:** Medical imaging, clinical decision support, hospital operations, patient engagement

## 3. Education & Training (Giáo Dục & Đào Tạo)

- **Description:** Personalised learning, optimising educational resources, and supporting learner pathway development.
- **Potential areas:** Adaptive learning platforms, AI tutoring, content generation, skills assessment

## 4. Disaster Prevention (Phòng Chống Thiên Tai)

- **Description:** Forecasting, modelling, and building response / recovery solutions for natural disasters.
- **Potential areas:** Early warning systems, damage assessment from satellite imagery, resource allocation, climate resilience planning

## 5. Innovation (Đổi Mới Sáng Tạo)

- **Description:** New product development, process improvement, and application of innovative methodologies.
- **Potential areas:** AI-enabled R&D, open innovation platforms, cross-sector AI applications

## 6. SME Productivity (Năng Suất Doanh Nghiệp Vừa & Nhỏ)

- **Description:** Build AI agents to automate operations and boost productivity for Small and Medium Enterprises.
- **Potential areas:** AI-powered business assistants, workflow automation, customer service bots, inventory/demand forecasting

## 7. Smart Government (Chính Phủ Thông Minh)

- **Description:** Solutions for smart cities and digital public services.
- **Potential areas:** Urban mobility, public service chatbots, administrative process automation, policy simulation

## 8. Agriculture (Nông Nghiệp)

- **Description:** Supply chain optimisation, precision farming, and climate adaptation in agriculture.
- **Potential areas:** Crop yield prediction, pest/disease detection, supply chain traceability, weather-adaptive farming

---

## Track Format

- Problem statements are drafted from **real enterprise needs**, not theoretical exercises
- Each track has an **enterprise partner** that provides the problem context
- Teams must produce a **working prototype (MVP)** within 48 hours
- Mentors and domain experts are assigned per track for guidance
- Post-competition: pilot opportunities with the sponsoring enterprise
