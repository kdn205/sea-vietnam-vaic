## Post-Competition Pathways

Beyond cash/credit, winning teams receive access to:

### Enterprise Track
- Direct connection with enterprise partners for piloting / co-development
- Proof-of-concept funding and acceleration

### National Track
- Showcase at **Vietnam Innovation Day** at NIC Hoa Lac
- Present on national stage to government and ecosystem partners

### Regional Track
- **Sponsored trip to NTU Singapore** for product development with AI Singapore
- Access to AI Singapore's network and mentorship

### Global Track
- **Silicon Valley connection** — networking with global tech ecosystem
- Exposure to international investors and partners
