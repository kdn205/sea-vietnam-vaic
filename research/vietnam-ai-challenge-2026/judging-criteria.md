## Evaluation Principles (from official event description)

VAIC 2026 distinguishes itself from traditional hackathons by focusing on:

1. **Real-world applicability** — does the solution solve an actual enterprise/social problem?
2. **AI-native architecture** — is AI at the core of the solution, not bolted on?
3. **Prototype completion** — can the team demonstrate a working MVP within 48 hours?
4. **Post-competition viability** — potential for piloting, scaling, or commercialisation
5. **Innovation & creativity** — novel approach to the problem statement

## Evaluation Process

### Phase 1: Application Review (pre-event)
- Teams submit profiles through the Hackathon Platform
- Selection based on skills, experience, and fit with challenge tracks
- 300+ teams selected from ~2,000 registrations

### Phase 2: 48-Hour Build (17–19 July)
- Teams develop AI-native prototypes on-site at FPT Tower
- Mentors and experts assigned per track
- Continuous feedback during the build

### Phase 3: Final Judging
- Presentations to the Judging Panel
- Live demonstrations of working prototypes
- Q&A with judges
